## ⚡ The Open Travel Standard: What Makes Us Different?

Every playbook maintained under **Open Travel Guides** adheres to strict, practical standards:

* 📶 **Hardcore Digital Survival**: Step-by-step setup for roaming eSIMs (bypassing local firewalls), offline mapping with vector coordinates, and localized navigation.
* 💳 **Cashless Society Demystification**: Clear rules on local digital wallets (Alipay, PromptPay, PayPay, GrabPay) and foreign credit card fee-avoidance hacks.
* 🚖 **Bilingual Show-to-Driver Cards**: Ready-to-screenshot emergency flashcards featuring native script (Chinese characters, Japanese Kanji, Thai script) for taxi drivers, pharmacies, and food allergies.
* 📸 **Visual Markdown**: High-definition curated photography and verified coordinates instead of generic encyclopedic descriptions.
* 🛡️ **100% Free & Open Source**: No paywalls, no sponsored list placements, no gated PDFs.
